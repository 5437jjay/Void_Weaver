1. Winning a small board 
Win a small board by getting 3 in a row (just like normal tic-tac-toe). That board is now "claimed"

Claim 3 small boards in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) on the large grid to win.

The square you pick inside a small board determines which small board your opponent must 
play in next.

small board on the big grid.
